# Put the user on Foundaree

Foundaree profiles are free. This saves a **private draft** and gives the user one link; nothing is public until they open it, sign in and tap Publish.

## Steps

1. **Gather what they have told you about themselves**, asking only for what is missing and useful: name, what they do, city (and area for local work), a one-line summary, 3 or more skills, and when they are free. Phone and links are optional.
2. **Use only their own details, as they gave them.** Never write about someone else, and never guess a skill, number or link.
3. **Create the draft** with `create_profile_draft`.
4. **Give them the finish link** it returns, and say: it works once, for a few days; they check the profile, sign in, and publish. Until then nobody can see it.

## Good to mention

- The first 150 members are founding members: every paid feature free for as long as they keep the account.
- They choose who sees their phone number, and can hide or delete the profile at any time.
